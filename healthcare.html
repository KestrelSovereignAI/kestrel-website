First deployment

Companion for the patient.
Navigator for the care team.

Kestrel is designed to support both sides of a human relationship: the person who needs support, and the human responsible for providing it. Healthcare is where we're proving that model first — through Kestrel-RCS, applied to remote patient monitoring.

What Kestrel-RCS does

Kestrel-RCS engages patients between clinical interactions, supports ongoing check-ins, and routes safety concerns to human care teams — without replacing clinical judgment.

FOR THE PATIENT
A companion that remembers.
Engages between clinical interactions, remembers context, and helps the patient stay connected to their care plan.
FOR THE CARE TEAM
A navigator that reduces the noise.
Supports ongoing patient engagement and surfaces concerns and context for the humans responsible for care — without replacing clinical judgment.
THE INFRASTRUCTURE BETWEEN THEM
Trust that doesn't belong to the model.
Identity, memory, and governance remain outside the underlying AI provider, keeping control with the people and organizations using Kestrel.
Real deployment

Live in a real clinical workflow.
